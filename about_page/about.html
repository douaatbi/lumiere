Who We Are

Founded in 2010, Lumiere began with a single vision: to honour the timeless art of jewelry-making.

Each piece is the result of weeks of deliberate craftsmanship — sourced from ethically mined gemstones, shaped by hands that love their art.

We believe jewellery is not merely decoration. It is memory, it is emotion, it is legacy.
